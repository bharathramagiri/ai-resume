How the AI Engine Works

1

Text Extraction

When you upload a PDF, our Python backend uses PyPDF2 to strip away complex formatting, tables, and fonts, leaving only the raw, readable data.

2

Vector Embeddings

Our SentenceTransformer AI model processes the resume and job description, converting the English text into high-dimensional numerical vectors.

3

Cosine Similarity

Scikit-Learn calculates the mathematical angle between the two vectors. The closer the vectors, the higher the semantic match percentage!
